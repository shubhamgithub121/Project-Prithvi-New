# Prithvi knowledge base

Everything the assistant may state as fact about Prithvi. If a question is not
answered here or by a tool, say you are not sure and point to support.

## About Prithvi
- Prithvi (formerly Plastic Revive) runs doorstep collection of segregated
  plastic waste in India, based in New Delhi.
- It started during the pandemic, when plastic waste from PPE kits, masks and
  disposables kept growing. Belief: "There is no such thing as AWAY."
- Prithvi has diverted more than 40,000 kg of plastic using value-based
  recycling. Vision: zero waste going to landfill.
- Collected plastic is processed through certified recycling facilities.
- Prithvi also sells products made from recycled material (see Eco Shop).

## Scheduling a pickup
- Page: /schedule-pickup (the user must be signed in).
- Step 1, address: search the address on the map or type it. City and a
  6-digit pincode are required.
- Step 2, schedule: pick a date (today or later, never in the past) and a
  time slot:
  - 8 AM - 12 PM
  - 12 PM - 4 PM
  - 4 PM - 8 PM
- Step 3, details: plastic type (optional) and estimated weight (minimum
  0.5 kg). The page shows estimated earnings before confirming.
- After confirming, the pickup appears under History on /profile with status
  "Scheduled".
- Editing a booked pickup is not supported yet. To change the date or slot,
  cancel it and book a new one.

## Accepted plastic
- PET: drink and water bottles
- HDPE: containers such as milk, shampoo and detergent bottles
- LDPE: plastic bags and films
- PP: packaging, tubs and caps
- Mixed plastic: choose "Mixed" if unsure or if items are combined
- Preparation tips: rinse out food and liquid, let it dry, flatten bottles and
  containers, keep plastic separate from wet or food waste.
- Not accepted: food waste, wet or dirty waste, glass, metal, paper, e-waste or
  hazardous/medical waste. If unsure about an item, contact support.

## Earnings
- Rate: Rs 15 per kg of plastic.
- Earnings shown when booking are estimates based on the weight the user
  enters (weight x Rs 15). Minimum weight is 0.5 kg.
- Earnings collect in the Eco Wallet on /profile (Rewards tab) and can be
  redeemed for Eco Shop items.
- Cancelled pickups do not count toward earnings or impact.
- Cash payout timing and method are not documented here. For those, direct
  the user to support.

## Eco tiers (based on total kg recycled)
- Sapling: 0 to under 5 kg
- Sprout: 5 to under 20 kg
- Tree: 20 to under 50 kg
- Forest Guardian: 50 kg and above
- Impact: every 1 kg recycled is counted as 2.5 kg of CO2 reduced on the
  profile page.
